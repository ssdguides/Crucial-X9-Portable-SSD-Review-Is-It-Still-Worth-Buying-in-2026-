How Fast Is the Crucial X9?

The Crucial X9 delivers impressive sequential transfer speeds for an everyday portable SSD. Published third-party benchmark results show read speeds around 950–1,050 MB/s under suitable testing conditions.

This makes it useful for transferring photos, videos, documents, and large backup files.

However, performance depends on your computer's USB port, cable, file sizes, and workload.

Is the Crucial X9 Worth Buying?

The Crucial X9 remains a capable portable SSD for everyday storage and backups. Its compact design and fast transfer speeds are appealing, especially for students, professionals, and content creators.

However, the standard X9 lacks an official dust or water resistance rating and hardware encryption. Its availability and pricing have also become important considerations following the discontinuation of Crucial's consumer business.

Before purchasing, compare its price with alternatives such as the Samsung T7 and Crucial X9 Pro.

Final Thoughts
